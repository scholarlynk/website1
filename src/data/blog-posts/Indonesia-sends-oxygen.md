Singapore, Malaysia and other countries in South East Asia are currently blanketed in a haze originating from Indonesia. Amidst the pollution, The Star reported the head of Indonesia's Government Communications Agency, Syaghanda Nainggolan, [as saying](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/12/we-send-oxygen-too-indonesian-officials-haze-remarks-spark-online-ridicule): 

"We're not just sending over the haze. When there's no haze, we send oxygen. The oxygen in Singapore and Malaysia comes from our forests, right?"
