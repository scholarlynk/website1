Amidst the haze that is blanketing Singapore, Malaysia and other countries in South East Asia and originating from Indonesia, The Star reported the head of Indonesia's Government Communications Agency, Syaghanda Nainggolan, saying(https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/12/we-send-oxygen-too-indonesian-officials-haze-remarks-spark-online-ridicule): "We're not just sending over the haze. When there's no haze, we send oxygen. The oxygen in Singapore and Malaysia comes from our forests, right?"
